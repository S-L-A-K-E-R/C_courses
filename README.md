# C Programming & Algorithmics

Welcome! I'm **SLAKER**, a programming teacher at engineering schools. This repository is a place for my students (and anyone else interested) to find useful material for learning **C programming and algorithmics**.

For now, it's **a collection of learning resources, not a complete course**. I've gathered material I find particularly helpful, along with exercises in English and French and an archive of previous class documents. I'll add my own structured course material as the repository develops.

## What you'll find here

- **[Exercises](EXERCISES/)** — Practice sheets in English and French, from variables and loops to functions, pointers, structures, files, sorting, and searching. There's also an additional exercise collection from Paris Dauphine.
- **[Archive](ARCHIVE/2026/)** — Older course and practical-work documents covering algorithmics and C. Useful for revising specific topics, though not organized as a new course.
- **[Syllabus](SYLLABUS.md)** — A planned overview of the course, still under construction.

## A little advice

Read through a topic, then **try the exercises yourself before looking at any corrections**. Compile often, pay attention to warnings, and don't be afraid to experiment: understanding why something *doesn't* work is part of learning to program.

Some of the resources collected here come from other authors or institutions. Please respect their credits and any reuse conditions stated in the original documents.

Whether you're preparing for a lab, revising for an exam, or simply learning C at your own pace, I hope these materials help you along the way.
